01 — How it works

Ten lookups at once. One writer. One critic.

LangGraph runs every research step in parallel, hands the results to a writer agent, and wires a writer ⇄ critic loop that keeps going until the plan passes — up to two rounds, keeping the best draft if a fix makes things worse.

02 — Why a critic agent

Language models write convincing plans that are quietly wrong.

Real mistakes from first drafts during testing — and what caught them.

First draft said
Budget total ₹28,850 of ₹30,000
The items actually added up to ₹36,950 — over budget.

First draft said
Day trip to Rohtang Pass in November
Closed to tourists from November to April.
Caught byVerified seasonal list
First draft said
Ganga Aarti at 1:30 AM
It happens at sunset.
Caught byCode · after midnight
First draft said
Overnight bus arrives 1:35 AM, then a full day of sightseeing
The next day's plans were filed under the travel day.
Caught byCode · overnight journeys
First draft said
Check out, 3 hours around South Goa, back by 7:30 PM
Not enough time to cover the distances.
Caught byAI reviewer
Layer 1

Rule checks in plain Python

Anything that can be counted: every day present, budget within limit, times in order, overnight journeys, enough time before a flight or train, nothing planned that's closed that month.

Layer 2

An AI reviewer

Judgement calls code can't make: realistic travel times, connections, contradictions between sections, and safety — like going above 3,500 m too soon.
